Apps

Four small, focused apps. Four very different problems.

RT Sullivan Consulting Ltd builds independent iPhone and iPad software — currently spanning livestock counting, card-magic memory training, perimenopause tracking and workout coaching. Different worlds, same habit: pick one problem and solve it properly.

The apps

Herdcount

£3.99 · iPhone & iPad

Counts chickens, sheep, cattle, plants and people in under a second — from a photo, or live as a mob walks through a gate. Self-correcting: pan back and the count unwinds instead of doubling, and an animal that turns around comes off the tally.

StackDrill

£4.99 · iPhone & iPad

A training app for magicians mastering a memorised deck. Ships with the Mnemonica and Aronson stacks built in — or add your own — and drills Card→Position, Position→Card, Neighbours, Sequence and ACAAN, with weighted selection that quietly resurfaces the cards you're weak on.

Menopause Intelligence

Free · iPhone, iPad & Apple Watch

Uses AI to predict high-symptom days before they happen, spotting the shifts in sleep quality, HRV and body temperature that precede hot flashes, brain fog, fatigue and mood changes. Connect an Apple Watch and it reads up to 90 days of existing Apple Health history, so it starts from real data rather than cold.

Sessionz.ai

Free · iPhone, iPad & Apple Watch

Builds your training week from your actual logged data and explains every choice — press 20kg for 8 this week and next week targets 20kg for 10. Reads Apple Health for resting heart rate, HRV and sleep, and only ever suggests weights for plates you actually own.
