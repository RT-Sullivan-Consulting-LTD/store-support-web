Getting started

Which stacks does StackDrill support?
Mnemonica and the Aronson stack are built in and ready to drill immediately. You can also add any custom stack of your own — enter the cards in your own order and StackDrill builds the drills around it.
How do memory hooks work?
Each deck position gets a Major System peg — a concrete image standing in for that number — and each card gets an image to help you picture it. These associations are a starting point: every one is editable, so you can swap in an image that sticks better for you.
What are the drill modes?
Card→Position, Position→Card, Neighbours, Sequence, ACAAN cuts, and a hands-free mode for practising without touching the screen.
Do I need an internet connection?
No. StackDrill runs entirely on your device.

Drilling and progress

Why do the same cards keep coming up?
StackDrill uses weighted selection: cards you get wrong are quietly resurfaced more often than cards you already know, so practice time goes where you need it. There's no setting to adjust — it happens automatically as you drill.
Can I edit an association after I've started drilling?
Yes, at any time. If a memory hook isn't working for you, replace it — your progress on that card isn't lost.
